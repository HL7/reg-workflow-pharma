### HL7 Vulcan Accelerator

[HL7 Vulcan](https://hl7vulcan.org/) is an HL7 FHIR Accelerator that brings together regulators, biopharmaceutical companies, healthcare organizations, technology vendors, academia, and patient groups. Its aim is to bridge clinical care and translational and clinical research by using HL7 FHIR for standardized data exchange across the medicinal product lifecycle.

### HL7 Biomedical Research and Regulation (BR&R)

The [HL7 Biomedical Research and Regulation Work Group](https://confluence.hl7.org/display/BRR) develops and maintains HL7 standards that support biomedical research and the regulatory evaluation of medicinal products. BR&R is the sponsoring work group for this Implementation Guide.
